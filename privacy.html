Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy applies to the DynastyWealth Wisdom publishing application (the "Application") and this website.

What the Application does

The Application is a personal automation tool used by the owner of the DynastyWealth Wisdom YouTube channel to prepare video files and upload them to the owner's own channel using the YouTube Data API. It is not distributed to, or operated on behalf of, any third party.

Data we access

  • Google account authorization for the channel owner's own account, limited to the YouTube Data API scopes required for uploading and managing the owner's own videos (youtube.upload, youtube, youtube.force-ssl, and read-only analytics).
  • Video files, titles, descriptions, and thumbnails created by the owner for upload to the owner's own channel.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store, or process personal data from viewers or any third parties.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or for training generalized AI models.

Storage & retention

Authorization tokens and video files are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Tokens can be revoked at any time via the Google Account permissions page, which immediately ends the Application's access.
